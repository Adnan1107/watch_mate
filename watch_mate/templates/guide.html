Comprehensive Watch Repair Tutorials

Battery Replacement: Learn how to safely remove and replace watch batteries.

  • Remove the watch back using the appropriate tool.
  • Carefully remove the old battery.
  • Check battery type and insert the new battery correctly.
  • Seal the watch back properly to maintain water resistance.

Strap Replacement: Tips for changing straps to refresh your watch style:

  • Remove the old strap using a spring bar tool.
  • Measure and choose the correct replacement strap.
  • Attach the new strap securely using spring bars.
  • Check for comfort and security before wearing.

Glass Replacement: How to replace cracked or scratched glass safely:

  • Open the watch case carefully.
  • Remove the damaged glass using proper tools.
  • Fit a new glass ensuring it sits flush.
  • Test water resistance and check sealing.

General Maintenance: Keep your watch running smoothly:

  • Clean the case, strap, and dial carefully.
  • Lubricate moving parts with proper oils.
  • Check for loose screws and tighten if needed.
  • Test functions after maintenance.

Common Tips & Safety:

  • Always use non-magnetic and watch-specific tools.
  • Work on a clean, soft surface to avoid scratches.
  • Handle delicate parts carefully to prevent damage.
  • Wear gloves to prevent fingerprints and dust inside the watch.
